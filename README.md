## About

I build applied AI products for healthcare, education, accessibility and civic participation. On every project I am both the **full-stack engineer and the AI engineer**: data model, APIs, interface, testing and deployment, plus model selection, prompt and agent design, evaluation and guardrails.

I care about the unglamorous parts that make AI safe to put in front of people: consent, privacy, human review, rate limits, testing and honest limitations.

Currently studying BSc Computer Science at the University of the People (President's List) and working as an independent Software & AI Engineer since 2024.
