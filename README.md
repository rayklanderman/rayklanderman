## About

I'm a **Software & AI Engineer based in Kenya** developing applied AI products for **healthcare, education, accessibility, civic participation, and research**.

I work across the software lifecycle—from **data models, APIs, frontend and mobile interfaces, testing, and deployment** to **model integration, prompt and agent design, evaluation, and safety guardrails**. I care about what happens beyond the demo: consent, privacy, accessibility, reliability, human review, and clear limitations.

- **Independent software and AI development since 2024**
- **Founder and lead developer** of AI Health Chat
- **BSc Computer Science (in progress)**, University of the People; **Diploma in ICT (Distinction)**, Management University of Africa
- **Open to** AI engineering, software engineering, machine learning, and cross-functional technical collaboration

> **Engineering principle:** Build useful systems, test their behavior, and make their limitations explicit.
